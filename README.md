## Make your own copy

1. Click **Fork** (top right) to copy this repo to your GitHub account.
2. Open `index.html` and find the block that says `EDIT THIS BLOCK`. Change the words in `PRESETS`.
3. In your fork, go to **Settings → Pages**, choose the **main** branch and the root folder, then **Save**.
4. In a minute or two your page is live at `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/usu-live-build/`.

No data is collected. Clicks are only counted on your own phone.
