# Hero video prompts (generate in Higgsfield or Gemini/Veo; save as assets/hero.mp4, 5-8 sec, loopable, no text in frame)

Keep it silent and under ~4 MB so it loads on campus wifi. Optional still: assets/hero-poster.jpg.

## Variant A: benefit (calm)
Early blue-hour morning in a Utah mountain-valley neighborhood, fresh snowfall, a clean-shoveled driveway and walkway glowing under a porch light, soft steam from a house chimney, slow push-in, cinematic, shallow depth of field, no people, no text.

## Variant B: humor
Handheld comedic shot of a college student in a beanie energetically shoveling a snowy driveway at sunrise, exaggerated determined face, snow spraying in slow motion, upbeat mood, warm morning light, mountains in the background, no text.

## Variant C: urgency
Time-lapse of heavy snow rapidly piling up on parked cars and a driveway at dusk, streetlights flickering on, dramatic dark-blue sky, tension building, locked-off wide shot, no people, no text.

## Class-opener clip (for the talk, ~10 sec)
Fast montage: crowded noisy city scene of overlapping billboards, phone notifications and ads, then a single calm, simple clean frame. Cinematic, no readable text.
