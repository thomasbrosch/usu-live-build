Your 30-Day Marketing Head Start

From Tom Broschinsky's talk in MSLE 3500 at Utah State, November 4, 2026. Everything here is free. Pick one thing and start this week.

Your 30-day plan

  • Week 1, Learn: pick one skill and finish one free course from the list below.
  • Week 2, Find: line up one real project: a club, a friend's business, a local shop or your own idea.
  • Week 3, Run: launch it with a small budget ($25 to $50) and track clicks, sign-ups and cost per result.
  • Week 4, Share: write a one-page case study (the goal, what you tried, the results in numbers, what you'd do next) and post it on LinkedIn.
  • Along the way: book one career coaching appointment and send one note to someone doing work you want, leading with something useful.

By December, you'll have real work to show.

Free courses and certifications

  • Google Skillshop: free Google Ads and Google Analytics training, with Google Ads certifications in Search, Display, Video, Shopping and Measurement.
  • Meta Blueprint: free courses on Facebook and Instagram ads. The Meta certification exams are separate, so check the exam price before you book one.
  • HubSpot Academy: free courses and certifications in inbound, social media, email, content and digital marketing.
  • Semrush Academy: free SEO and digital marketing courses.
  • Canva Design School: design basics for social posts and ads.
  • AI Fluency for Students (Claude Academy): using AI to learn, explore careers, improve your resume and practice interviews while keeping your own voice.
  • Huntsman's free "Dare Mighty Careers" courses: self-guided career prep from the Huntsman School.

Where to find work

At USU

  • Huntsman Career Development: one-on-one coaching by major for resumes, LinkedIn, interviews and offers. Resume group appointments Fridays at 1 PM, plus Focused Fridays, the Career Expo and Mock Interview Day.
  • USU Career Design Center (University Inn 102, Mon to Fri 8 to 5): open to every major, with resume and cover letter drop-ins, internships and career assessments.
  • Aggie Handshake: USU's job and internship board. Sign in with your A-number.

Beyond campus

  • jobs.utah.gov: Utah's state job board, with 35,000+ openings and virtual job fairs.
  • Wellfound: startup jobs, free for candidates.
  • Contra and Upwork: build a freelance profile and find paid projects.
  • LinkedIn: find USU alumni doing work you want and follow up on what you learn from them.

Copy-paste AI prompts

Use these with Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Fill in the [brackets]. Always check the answer, because your name goes on it. Take your phone number and address off anything you paste.

Start from the live demo

The class demo (the Cache Valley Snow Crew page) is in this repo. Its headline, offer and button all live in one block of text at the top of index.html, called PRESETS. These two prompts use it.

Remix the demo for your own idea

Here is the PRESETS block from a class demo page: [paste the PRESETS block from index.html]. It has three versions: a (benefit), b (humor) and c (urgency). Rewrite all three for [my club / my friend's business / my idea], aimed at [who I want to reach]. Keep each headline under 8 words. Keep the same format so I can paste it back in. Don't invent prices, results or claims I didn't give you.

Plan your Week 3 test

I'm running a small test for [my project] with $[25 to 50]. I have two or three versions of my message (like the a, b and c versions in the class demo). Help me plan it: what I'm trying to learn, who sees each version, how long to run it, and which numbers to track (clicks, sign-ups, cost per result). Then give me a simple table to fill in. Ask me questions if anything is unclear. Don't guess numbers for me.

Use in any chatbot

Learn a marketing concept

Teach me [concept, like cost per acquisition, positioning or A/B testing] like I'm a college sophomore. Give me a one-paragraph explanation, an example from a small local business, and 3 quiz questions. Wait for my answers before you grade them.

Review your resume or LinkedIn

Act as a recruiter hiring for [type of role]. Here is my [resume / LinkedIn About section]: [paste]. Give me the 5 changes that would help most, rewrite my weakest bullet to show a result with a number, and flag anything that sounds generic. Keep my voice.

Search for jobs and internships

I'm a [year] at Utah State majoring in [major]. I want [an internship / part-time work / an entry-level job] in [field] around [Logan / Salt Lake / remote] for [semester]. Give me 10 job titles to search that I might not know, the keywords to use on Handshake, and 3 skills I could show in a small project to stand out.

Find a local freelance client

I want to help a small business in Cache Valley with [social media / ads / email]. List 10 kinds of local businesses that often need this help and what each one probably struggles with. Then write a short, friendly message offering to run it free for one month in exchange for a reference and permission to share the results.

Reach out to someone

I want to reach out to [name], a [role] at [company]. Here's what I know about their work: [paste]. Write a 3-sentence LinkedIn message that leads with a useful idea or a sharp question, not a request for a job.

Write your case study

Here are my notes from a marketing project: [goal, what I did, the numbers]. Turn them into a one-page case study with four parts: the goal, what I tried, the results in numbers, and what I'd do next. Then write a short LinkedIn post about it. Don't add any numbers I didn't give you.
